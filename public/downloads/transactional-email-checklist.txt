FICTIONAL WORKED EXAMPLE
12 saved bookings; one recipient and one initial confirmation each. All requests accepted by the provider at the review cutoff, 09:10 UTC on 12 November 2026.
9 recipient-server acceptances + 2 temporary delays + 1 permanent failure = 12 initial messages.
Of the 9 accepted messages, controlled mailbox inspection finds 8 in the inbox + 1 in junk = 9. Folder observations are a subset, not extra messages.
2 delays + 1 failure + 1 junk placement = 4 cases to investigate. All 12 bookings remain confirmed. Resolve issues and repeat affected checks before sign-off.
These invented counts and times are neither client results nor delivery benchmarks. Retain the cutoff; later events add to the history. No future inbox-placement rate is inferred.
