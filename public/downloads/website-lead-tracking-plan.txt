ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE — NOT CLIENT RESULTS OR A BENCHMARK
Arrival cohort: stored website-form records received 1–31 October 2026, UTC.
Outcome cutoff: 10 November 2026, 09:00 UTC.
40 stored records - 4 tests - 6 spam - 5 duplicate follow-ups = 25 distinct enquiries.
25 distinct enquiries = 10 qualified + 8 not a fit + 7 awaiting review.
10 qualified = 4 won + 3 lost + 3 still open.
Qualified share: 10 / 25 x 100 = 40%.
Won share of enquiries: 4 / 25 x 100 = 16%.
Won share of qualified: 4 / 10 x 100 = 40%.
Assumes at most one signed engagement per enquiry. Older enquiries won in November, phone enquiries and direct-email enquiries are excluded. Pending cases remain visible; these rates are snapshots, not forecasts.
