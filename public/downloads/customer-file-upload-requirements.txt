3. ORIGINAL EXAMPLE — REPLACE WITH YOUR POLICY

Fictional project P41 accepts nonempty PDF/JPEG/PNG files up to and including 10,000,000 bytes each. 1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes. Independent single-file requests; project count/storage quotas not reached; no combined request cap involved. No replacements, compression or conversion. Both PDFs pass format and size checks. The JPEG is rejected before a durable original is retained.

artwork.pdf | 8 MB | all required checks pass | 8 MB retained original | available
brief.pdf   | 2 MB | scanner times out        | 2 MB retained original | held, inaccessible
logo.jpg    | 12 MB | exceeds per-file limit  | 0 MB retained original | rejected

At the snapshot: 3 offered = 1 available + 1 held + 1 rejected.
Offered payload: 8 + 2 + 12 = 22 MB.
Retained original payload: 8 + 2 = 10 MB; available payload: 8 MB.
Exclude buffers, logs, backups, replicas and metadata; these are not billing or traffic figures.
If the same held brief later passes: 2 files / 10 MB available; logo remains rejected.
Business readiness is a separate decision if a required document is still missing.

A. Mixed outcomes: reproduce P41; independently verify all three file outcomes and project readiness.
B. Boundaries: 0 bytes, exactly 10,000,000 bytes, and 10,000,001 bytes under the example policy. Passing size alone does not pass other checks.
C. Wrong format: supplier-prepared harmless fixture with a misleading filename; verify server-side rejection under the agreed content rules.
D. Scanner rejection: approved harmless scanner test fixture or safely simulated result; no live malware.
E. Delayed check: held and inaccessible, with an owner; retry screening without demanding another customer upload.
F. Interrupted transfer: check cleanup and per-file retry; preserve successful files.
G. Lost acknowledgement: complete receipt but disconnect before the browser learns it; look up the attempt before retrying, without duplicate attachments.
H. Replacement/removal: a late check result cannot release different bytes or recreate a deleted file.
I. Access: direct download, preview and other retrieval routes as another customer, signed out, and after access removal. Record actual time-limited-link behavior.
J. Keyboard/status: choose without dragging, reach every action, identify affected file/control, and demonstrate assistive-technology status feedback.

For each case record an independent stored-state/retrieval check, not only a banner. Agree support escalation, open defects, release decision and retest evidence with the responsible owner.
