Acceptance rehearsal
Open the new-language entry URL in a fresh session with the browser in the existing language. Verify the requested page language.
Switch each way on a paired page. Verify the equivalent destination.
Follow a labelled link to a deliberately untranslated page and verify the way back.
Submit invalid input, correct it, and verify the rest of the input is still available.
In a test environment, simulate a failure before saving and verify the alternative contact route.
Submit a labelled enquiry. Match the on-page confirmation and receipt against the saved record and confirm who will respond.
Repeat the route at a narrow viewport and using a keyboard. Review the copy in context with a fluent reviewer.
