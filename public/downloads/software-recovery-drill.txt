ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE — NOT AN INDUSTRY BENCHMARK

Fictional booking company, database unavailable; same date, 23 October 2026, UTC.
Agreed RPO: 60 minutes. Agreed RTO: 120 minutes.
Latest usable recovered state: 10:00. Interruption: 11:20.
Agreed minimum service usable: 12:50, allowing controlled reconciliation afterwards.
Data gap: 80 minutes — FAIL, 20 minutes beyond RPO.
Recovery time: 90 minutes — PASS, 30 minutes inside RTO.
Sequential assumed phases: 15 minutes detection/authorization/access + 45 minutes restore + 30 minutes validation = 90 minutes.
Overall: remediation required; faster restoration alone does not fix the stale recovery point.
At an assumed uniform 12 new bookings/hour, 12 x 80 / 60 = 16 new bookings in the gap. This is exposure, not confirmed loss, and excludes edits/cancellations/files and new requests during the outage.
