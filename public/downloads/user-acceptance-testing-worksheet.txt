6. WORKED EXAMPLE — FICTIONAL TRAINING BOOKING TOOL

All prices, rules and results below are illustrative assumptions, not client results or market benchmarks. Quantity is a positive whole number; USD 80 per attendee; a 10% discount applies to the entire booking at 10 or more attendees. Amendments must reapply the rule. Taxes, fees, payment collection, capacity limits and cancellation rules are excluded from this narrow exercise. Prices avoid fractional cents; other prices/discounts require an agreed rounding rule.

Formula for 1–9 attendees: quantity x USD 80.
Formula for 10 or more attendees: quantity x USD 80 x 0.90.

UAT-01 — Create 9 attendees.
Expected: 9 x USD 80 = USD 720.
Observed: shows and saves USD 720. PASS.

UAT-02 — Create 10 attendees.
Expected: 10 x USD 80 x 0.90 = USD 720.
Observed: shows and saves USD 720. PASS.

UAT-03 — Create 11 attendees.
Expected: 11 x USD 80 x 0.90 = USD 792.
Observed: shows and saves USD 792. PASS.

UAT-04 — Try 0 attendees.
Expected: explain invalid quantity and prevent saving.
Observed: error shown; no booking saved. PASS.

UAT-05 — Amend a saved booking from 10 attendees to 9.
Expected: remove discount and save USD 720.
Observed: retains discount and saves USD 648. FAIL.

UAT-06 — Reopen the booking from UAT-05.
Expected: 9 attendees and USD 720.
Observed: 9 attendees and USD 648. FAIL.

UAT-07 — Create a fresh 9-person booking using only the keyboard.
Expected: reach controls, see focus, save USD 720.
Observed: tested flow completed with visible focus. PASS.
This is not a comprehensive accessibility assessment.

UAT-08 — Send confirmation for UAT-01.
Expected: approved test inbox receives 9 attendees and USD 720.
Observed: test inbox connection unavailable; receipt cannot be checked safely. BLOCKED.

Initial results: 5 passed + 2 failed + 1 blocked = 8 scenarios.
The two failures may share one stale-discount defect; investigation must establish the cause.
Incorrect amended total: 9 x USD 80 x 0.90 = USD 648.
Difference: USD 720 - USD 648 = USD 72 below the expected total for this one booking, not a revenue forecast.
Both fresh 9-person and 10-person bookings total USD 720 because of the whole-booking discount at 10.

Agreed rule for this example: customer prices must be correct and confirmation delivery must be demonstrated.
Initial decision: HOLD this release. Wrong price is a release blocker; confirmation evidence is missing.

Illustrative follow-up: supplier identifies a fixed version; team connects the isolated test inbox and reruns all eight scenarios. All eight match expectations, including actual receipt in the test inbox. Preserve initial findings and attach fresh evidence: 8 passes within this defined test scope. Production checks remain separate.

This is a deliberately incomplete teaching example. Add relevant cases such as negative/fractional/blank quantities, unauthorized users, large groups and interrupted saves before treating your own plan as complete. Identify rounding, tax, payment and other real business rules separately. Passing these eight scenarios cannot approve all possible bookings or production operation.
